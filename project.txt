Project Title :- CareerConnect-Professional Networking and Job Portal

Problem Statement :- 
    Nowadays, job seekers often use different websites to create professional profiles and search for job opportunities, while 
    companies need an easy way to post jobs and manage applicants. Many existing platforms have too many features, making them 
    complex for learning and academic purposes. The aim of this project is to develop a simple web-based Professional Networking 
    and Job Portal called CareerConnect, where users can create and manage their profiles, add education, skills, and experience, 
    search and apply for jobs, and companies can post job openings and view applications. This project will also help demonstrate 
    the practical use of database management, backend development, and frontend technologies in a real-world application.

Purpose :- 
    To provide an online platform where job seekers and companies can interact efficiently for recruitment while maintaining
     professional information in a structured database.
